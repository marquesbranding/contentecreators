## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Static tips are shown to creators browsing the catalog
The catalog page SHALL display a static section of guidance tips for creators (e.g. keeping their profile updated, following up with companies, staying safe), with fixed content requiring no backend data.

#### Scenario: Creator views the catalog page
- **WHEN** an authenticated creator views the catalog page
- **THEN** a tips section with at least the "Atualize suas informações", "Aproveite as marcas", and "Segurança sempre" tiles is visible
