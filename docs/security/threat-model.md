# Beta threat model

This model covers the security boundaries required before hosted development
and production. It contains no real identity, credential, participant, or
provider payload.

## Review rules

- Re-run the linked automated evidence whenever the boundary changes.
- Treat every Server Action and Route Handler as a directly callable entry
  point.
- Keep authorization in the server DAL/service and RLS; UI visibility and
  Proxy redirects are not authorization.
- Record new unresolved High/Critical findings as launch blockers. Do not
  silently accept them.

### TM-PUBLIC-01 — public profile photos (client decision, 2026-09-10)

The client approved showing creator profile photos on the anonymous landing
page. That widens the surface `TM-PUBLIC-01` previously closed entirely, so
the control is scoped rather than removed:

- Only creators the backoffice marked `is_featured` carry a photo. Every other
  approved creator renders initials, so nobody is published by the mere fact of
  being approved.
- The `profile-media` bucket stays private. Delivery mirrors sponsorship
  creatives: the service role signs one object at a time over RLS, and no
  authenticated code path is loosened.
- The DTO still carries no contact channel, no social handle and no detail
  route, so a photo cannot be turned into a way to reach the person off-platform.
- The signature lifetime must exceed the route's `s-maxage` +
  `stale-while-revalidate` window, or a cached response serves dead URLs.
  Regression-tested in `public-community-proof.handler.unit.test.ts`.

Open launch gates owned outside the codebase:

- Terms of Use still placeholder (`ptBR.legal.launchBlocker`); the public
  display of approved profiles must be described there.
- The signup consent text now states that curated profiles may be featured
  publicly. Accounts approved under the previous wording are covered by the
  `is_featured` restriction — curation implies prior contact — but the client
  owns the decision to keep it that way or seek fresh consent before widening.

Note: `publicSocialProofEnabled` remains `false` and is unrelated to this
section — it gates whether _participant-derived sponsorship creatives_ are
shown to anonymous viewers (`isSponsorshipCreativeVisible`), and is enforced at
deployment by `hosted-deployment-target.ts`.

## Review outcome

No unresolved High or Critical finding is accepted by this model, except the
scoped, client-approved public photo exposure recorded under `TM-PUBLIC-01`
above. Operational verification in the target environment, client-owned legal
inputs, and final credentials remain separate launch gates.
